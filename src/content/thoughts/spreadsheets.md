Every well-kept sheet is a letter to someone you will never meet: the colleague who opens it next spring, looking for one number, and finds it exactly where it should be. Nobody thanks a spreadsheet. But the frozen header row, the column that says which unit it is in, the small note on the tab that explains the odd month: these are courtesies, left in the dark for whoever comes next. <mark class="tbd">[draft]</mark>
